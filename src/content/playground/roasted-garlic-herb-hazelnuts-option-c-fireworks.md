This is Option C, the last of three label concepts I designed for a hazelnut product for Oregon State University's Food Science Department. Like **Option A** and **Option B**, the page opens on the bag before it has a label. It's just a ziplock bag and nuts.

Everything on the label waits in small knots on the shelf underneath, lined up in the order I stacked the layers in Illustrator. Run your finger or cursor along the shelf from left to right, and each knot you push climbs, bursts, and flies into place.

- **Set them all off** launches every knot without you.
- **Pack it up again** squeezes the label back down onto the shelf.

This one is the simplest of the three: flat shapes, no photograph, no halftone. The hazelnuts, garlic cloves and herb leaves are scattered across a black panel, and the ones at the edges are trimmed off by the panel, the way a pattern runs off a printed label. Each piece flies in already trimmed and lands exactly where it sits on the bag.

## Where this comes from

Same machinery as **Samurai Fireworks** and the other two options. The order comes from my own Illustrator layers. Claude and I built it in an afternoon.
