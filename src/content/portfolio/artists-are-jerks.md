<p><strong>Where can you see real art by local artists, in person, this week?</strong></p><p>In the Rogue Valley, the usual answer is "ask around." Artists Are Jerks answers it properly: a running list of showings, studios and places with art on the walls, so people can go stand in front of the work.</p><p>Behind the scenes it's more than a list. Artists sign in to upload their work and build their pages, and venues post dated showings. The front door is a cartoon studio, because a directory doesn't have to look like a spreadsheet.</p><p>My own project, designed and built from scratch.</p><p><a href="https://artistsarejerks.com" class="cta-link">Visit artistsarejerks.com →</a></p>
