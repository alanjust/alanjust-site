<p><strong>Why does one painting stop you cold while the one next to it doesn't?</strong></p><p>Seeing has a grammar. Nobody teaches it, but your eye follows it anyway. I explain it in museum galleries as a docent, and Hidden Grammar of Art is that tour, written down. Each work gets a plain-language read of what your eye does before you've decided what you're looking at. No art-school jargon.</p><p>My own project. I designed it, wrote it and built it, including the AI-assisted analysis underneath, which checks every work against the same fixed set of visual principles.</p><p><a href="https://hiddengrammarofart.com" class="cta-link">Visit hiddengrammarofart.com →</a></p>
