Spiral Poem

Follow the spiral in from the outside.

Lettering

The spiral is the sentence pulled tight. Trace it with a finger or a cursor and the words stand up out of the line, outside in, so it ends at the center.

Reach is how much of the line wakes up around your finger. Linger is how long the words stay before sinking; all the way right, they stay until you press Sink it all.

The letters are the Hershey fonts, drawn as single pen lines for plotters by Dr. A. V. Hershey at the U.S. National Bureau of Standards. The font data format was created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc.
