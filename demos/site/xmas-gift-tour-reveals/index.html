A test page. Rise is the way the story has worked so far: each scene stretches up off the line. Unfold opens each scene sideways like a book, following your finger; whatever you haven’t reached yet waits just ahead of it, folded and jumbled, and comes into focus as you pass. The hints show a first-time visitor what to do and disappear the moment they touch the line.

The lettering is small this way. Turn your phone sideways and the story reads better.
