Phil, in four inks

A portrait of my friend Phil, built the way a printing press builds color: four sheets of squares, one for each ink, each tilted to its own angle. Here are three ways to take him apart and put him back together.

One

The plates, pulled apart

Turn the stack and pull the four plates apart in space. Flip from ink on paper to light in the dark to see subtractive and additive color do the same job from opposite directions.

Two

Out of register

Be the bad press. Each plate is on its own spring, so a thumb on the sheet or a shake of the press peels the colors apart, and they wobble back into line.

Three

Hand-colored

He starts in black and white. Drag through him to bring the color back, or pick a new hue and repaint him a square at a time. What you paint stays until you reset.

Works with a mouse, a finger or an Apple Pencil.
