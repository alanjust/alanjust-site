Roasted Garlic Herb Hazelnuts

A bag of hazelnuts, and a label that has to be talked into existing.

The bag starts bare. Everything that makes it a product (the pictures, the orange bands, the words) has been squeezed into small knots on the shelf, lined up in the order the label was built in Illustrator: the bottom layer first, the type last. Run your finger or cursor along the shelf from left to right. Each knot you push climbs, and when it reaches its height it bursts, and its pieces fly out to where they belong on the bag.

So a single sweep builds the label from the bottom of the stack to the top, the same way a designer would.
