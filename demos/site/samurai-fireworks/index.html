Samurai Fireworks

A drawing by Jeff Johnson, packed into a few knots of ink.

The drawing’s pen strokes are sorted into groups by where they sit, a head here, a sword there, and each group is squeezed into one small knot on the line, right under the part of him it becomes. Run your finger or cursor along the line from left to right. Each knot you push climbs, and the farther you push, the higher it goes. When it reaches its height, it bursts, and its strokes fly out to where they belong.

A group that travels together and arrives together is what mathematicians call a hyperedge. The groups here were found by the computer, not drawn by Jeff. Color the groups shows you where it drew the lines.
