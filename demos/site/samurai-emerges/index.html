Samurai Emerges

A drawing by Jeff Johnson, folded down into a single line.

Version

Every pen stroke in the drawing is lying flat on that line, right under where it belongs. Rub the line and whatever stands above that spot gets up: a foot, a sleeve, the sword. Stop, and after a moment he lies back down.

Reverse Jenga stands each stroke up whole, so he goes together piece by piece. Accordion keeps every line connected and stretches him up out of the line like a folded paper fan opening, fully open under your cursor and tapering off to either side.

Reach is how wide a slice your cursor wakes up. Linger is how long he stays up before sinking.
