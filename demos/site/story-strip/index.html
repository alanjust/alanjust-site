Stand-in art: Jeff Johnson's samurai, three times over, until the real panels arrive.

Every outline in the drawing is squashed flat onto the line, right under where it belongs. Rubbing stretches it straight up, so nothing slides sideways and the ink never tangles. Once a panel is up it stays up. At the end, the button lays the whole story back down and rolls you to the start.
